A trajectory planner tool using Hohmann Transfer mechanic to move a space craft from earth to the moon. 

https://jf0x7.github.io/OrbitalMechanics//

Key terminology: 

Hohmann transfer is the most fuel-efficient way to move a spacecraft between two circular orbits around the same body, using just two engine burns.

Delta-V: Change in velocity. ΔV=Vfinal​−Vinitial​

Periapsis: The closest point of an orbit to the central body (the body being orbited).

Apoapsis: The farthest point of an orbit from the central body.
